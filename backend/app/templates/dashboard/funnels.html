How Funnel Tracking Works

What is a Funnel?

A funnel tracks the sequential steps users take through a specific flow on your website. It helps you identify where users drop off and optimize your conversion rates.

Example Funnel: E-commerce Checkout

Step 1: Product Page → /product (100% - 1000 users)
Step 2: Add to Cart → /cart (60% - 600 users)
Step 3: Checkout → /checkout (35% - 350 users)
Step 4: Confirmation → /order-complete (25% - 250 users)

Automatic Tracking

Funnels are tracked automatically using pageviews. Once you create a funnel with URL paths, we'll automatically track visitors as they navigate through each step. No additional code needed!

Key Metrics:

  • Conversion Rate: Percentage of initial visitors who complete each step
  • Drop-off Rate: Percentage of users who leave between steps
  • Total Visitors: Number of unique visitors who entered the funnel
